# WASSCE Prep Ghana: Syllabus Tracker, Offline Pasco, Study Squad & Gemini AI Textbook Guide

A comprehensive, offline-first study companion tailored for Ghanaian Senior High School (SHS) students to master the WAEC/WASSCE syllabus, drill past questions (Pasco) under timed exam conditions, collaborate with classmates, and upload PDF textbooks/notes to generate personalized Gemini AI study guides, summaries, and revision questions.

## User Review & Critical Decisions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Revised Core Capabilities & Decisions**:
> - **PDF Textbook Import & Gemini AI Study Guides**: Students can import PDF textbooks, course handouts, or class notes using Android's system document picker. Powered by Gemini (`gemini-3.8-flash`), the app analyzes the textbook chapters to generate structured WASSCE-aligned study guides, key definition cheat sheets, formula summaries, examiner focus notes, and custom chapter practice quizzes.
> - **Offline Caching of AI Guides**: Once generated by Gemini, all study guides, summaries, and flashcards are cached in the local Room database so students can study them in boarding school prep or offline without internet.
> - **Track & Subject Coverage**: Built for all major Ghanaian SHS programmes (Core subjects: English, Core Maths, Integrated Science, Social Studies; Electives: Elective Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Government, Economics, Financial Accounting).
> - **Study Squad & Battles**: Live quiz battles with countdown timers, virtual study rooms (synchronized Pomodoro timer & presence), peer challenges, and school/classmate leaderboards.
> - **Offline Past Questions (Pasco)**: Timed WAEC Paper 1 exam simulation and topic-by-topic practice with step-by-step working and marking scheme tips.

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

### What It Does
**WASSCE Prep Ghana** gives Ghanaian SHS students an all-in-one preparation engine:
1. **Gemini AI Textbook Study Companion**: Add PDF textbooks and revision materials. Gemini parses the chapter and generates:
   - High-yield chapter summaries aligned with the WAEC syllabus.
   - Core concepts, definitions, and mathematical/scientific derivations.
   - "Chief Examiner Warnings" on common student mistakes for that topic.
   - Auto-generated chapter practice questions with step-by-step solutions.
2. **Official WASSCE Syllabus Tracker**: Track completion percentage across all syllabus units with status toggles (`Not Started`, `Revising`, `Mastered`) and exam weight indicators.
3. **Offline Pasco Hub**: Access authentic past questions (2015–2024) with timed mock exam simulation, topic drills, bookmarking, and instant scoring.
4. **Study Squad & Classmate Arena**: Real-time quiz battles, virtual study prep rooms with focus timers, friend status, and school leaderboards.
5. **Readiness Analytics**: Visual radar charts showing syllabus mastery versus exam performance per subject.

### Target Audience & Persona
- **Ghanaian SHS Candidates (SHS 1–3)**: Preparing for WASSCE (May/June school candidates and Nov/Dec private candidates) across Ghanaian secondary schools (e.g. PRESEC Legon, Achimota, Wesley Girls, Prempeh College, Mfantsipim, Holy Child, Opoku Ware).
- **Study Context**: Students revising in dormitories, prep halls with no Wi-Fi, or home study groups.

## 2. User Experience & Visual Design

### Key User Flows
1. **PDF Textbook Import & AI Study Guide Flow**:
   - Tap **"Add Textbook / Notes"** in the Study Guides tab.
   - Select any PDF document (e.g., *Aki-Ola Elective Mathematics*, *Ababio Chemistry*, *Integrated Science for SHS*, or class handouts) using Android's zero-permission system document picker.
   - The app extracts the selected chapter or pages and presents study guide generation options:
     - **Complete Chapter Summary & Key Concepts**
     - **Formula & Definition Flashcards**
     - **WASSCE Exam Focus & Common Pitfalls**
     - **Generate 10-Question Diagnostic Quiz**
   - Gemini streams the generated study guide in real time. The student can save it to their offline library, star key sections, or export to study flashcards.
2. **Syllabus Mastery Tracker**:
   - View overall WASSCE readiness percentage.
   - Filter by Core or Elective subjects and view unit breakdowns.
   - Toggle topic status (`Not Started`, `Reading`, `Mastered`) with instant progress recalculation.
3. **Offline Pasco Drill & Mock Exam Engine**:
   - Choose between **Timed Mock Exam** (full 50-question countdown replicating WAEC Paper 1) and **Topical Practice**.
   - Review answers with step-by-step working, diagrams, and examiner marking scheme notes.
4. **Study Squad & Live Battle Arena**:
   - Jump into rapid 5-question quiz duels against classmates or intelligent peer study bots.
   - Join virtual prep rooms with shared Pomodoro timers and classmate study statuses.
   - View weekly XP leaderboards segmented by class and school.

### Visual Identity & Theme

  - **Colors**: Rich Ghana Gold / Amber (`#F59E0B`), Forest Emerald (`#065F46` / `#10B981`), and Deep Regal Slate (`#0B0F19` dark / `#F8FAFC` light).
  - **Accents**: Vibrant Coral (`#F43F5E`) for battle timers, Warm Cyan (`#06B6D4`) for AI study guides, and Golden Badges for quiz masteries.
- **Typography & Formatting**:
  - Bold academic display headlines, clean body typography with proper spacing, and monospace numbers for math formulas and exam countdowns.
- **Component Styling & Layout**:
  - Smooth M3 Navigation Bar: **Home**, **Syllabus**, **Pasco Practice**, **AI Textbooks**, **Study Squad**.
  - Markdown-styled AI study guide view with collapsible sections, copy/bookmark actions, and formula callout boxes.

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

### Decision 1: Gemini AI Integration via Firebase AI Logic (`gemini-3.8-flash`)
- **Chosen Approach**: Use Firebase AI Logic with `gemini-3.8-flash` to process textbook chapters and generate streaming, structured study guides.
- **Why**: `gemini-3.8-flash` provides fast, low-latency reasoning on academic STEM and humanities texts without requiring paid Blaze billing for text generation. Streaming output (`generateContentStream`) ensures immediate visual feedback while processing long chapters.
- **Offline Caching**: Once generated, study guides are stored in Room Database, enabling offline access forever without recurring network or API calls.

### Decision 2: Zero-Permission PDF Document Picking & Text Extraction
- **Chosen Approach**: Use Android's standard `ActivityResultContracts.OpenDocument` (Storage Access Framework) to let users pick their PDF textbooks safely without requiring broad storage permissions (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` is strictly avoided per Google Play policy).
- **Text Extraction**: Read and extract text blocks from the PDF URI using Android platform streams and `PdfRenderer`, passing chapter excerpts to Gemini with prompt engineering tailored to WAEC curriculum standards.

### Decision 3: Offline-First Syllabus & Past Questions Bank
- **Chosen Approach**: Ship comprehensive pre-populated WASSCE syllabus data and real past question papers for Core and Elective subjects in local Room DB and asset JSONs.
- **Why**: Guarantees core exam revision never fails even during school power outages or zero mobile data connectivity.

### Core Entities & State
1. **`TextbookDocument`**: `id`, `title`, `subjectId`, `fileName`, `fileUriString`, `pageCount`, `addedDateTimestamp`.
2. **`AiStudyGuide`**: `id`, `textbookId`, `subjectId`, `chapterTitle`, `summaryMarkdown`, `keyConceptsJson`, `examFocusPointsJson`, `generatedQuestionsJson`, `createdAtTimestamp`.
3. **`Subject` & `Topic`**: Full WASSCE syllabus tree with completion status, confidence ratings, and exam weights.
4. **`PastQuestion` & `ExamAttempt`**: WASSCE past questions with answers, explanations, timer records, and grade estimation.
5. **`StudyBuddy` & `StudyRoom`**: Collaborative peer squad, live battle challenges, and focus timers.

### Verification & Testing
- Automated Robolectric tests covering:
  - Syllabus progress calculation and mastery state transitions.
  - Pasco exam grading, time tracking, and grade classification (A1–F9).
  - Study guide caching and retrieval in Room DB.
  - Study squad battle scoring and leaderboard updates.
